My app - Account Deletion

Request Account Deletion

If you want to delete your My app account and associated data, you can request account deletion by contacting us using the email address provided below.

How to Request Deletion

  1. Send an email to our support email address.
  2. Use "Account Deletion Request" as the email subject.
  3. Include the email address associated with your My app account.
  4. We will verify the request and process the deletion.
Important: Please make sure you provide the email address that is associated with your My app account so that we can identify your account.

Data That Will Be Deleted

When your account deletion request is processed, the following account-related information will be deleted, where applicable:

  • Your account information
  • Your name and email address
  • Your profile information
  • Saved account data
  • Orders associated with your account

Data That May Be Retained

Some information may need to be retained when required by law, for fraud prevention, security, accounting, or other legal requirements.

Any information that must be retained will only be kept for the period required by applicable laws or regulations.

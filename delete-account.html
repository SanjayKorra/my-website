My app - Account Deletion

This page explains how users of My app can request deletion of their account and associated data.

How to Request Account Deletion

If you want to delete your My app account and associated personal data, please send an account deletion request to:

Email: sevenstarsanju1@gmail.com

Please use the subject line: Account Deletion Request - My app

In your request, please provide the email address associated with your My app account so that we can identify the account that you want to delete.

What Happens After Your Request

  1. We will review your account deletion request.
  2. We may verify that the request belongs to the account owner.
  3. Once verified, your account will be deleted.
  4. Personal data associated with the account will be deleted unless it is required to be retained for legal, security, fraud-prevention, or other legitimate purposes.

Data That Will Be Deleted

When your account is deleted, the following account-related information will generally be deleted:

  • User account information
  • Name associated with the account
  • Email address associated with the account
  • User profile information
  • Account-related points or score information
  • Other personal information associated with your account

Data That May Be Retained

Certain information may need to be retained for a limited period when required by law, to resolve disputes, prevent fraud or abuse, maintain security, or comply with other legal obligations.

If any information must be retained, it will only be kept for the period necessary for the applicable legal or legitimate purpose.

Important: Deleting your account may permanently remove your account and associated information. Account deletion may not be reversible.

Account Deletion Time

We will process valid account deletion requests within a reasonable period after receiving and verifying the request.

Some data that must be retained for legal or security reasons may remain for the required retention period before being permanently deleted.

Need Help?

If you have questions about account deletion or your data, please contact us using the email address above.
